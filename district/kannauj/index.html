📅 Kannauj Sampoorna Samadhan Diwas Notice

In Kannauj district, Sampoorna Samadhan Diwas is held at tehsil headquarters on designated Saturdays (check your local tehsil for the current Saturday schedule). If your grievance is unresolved after 15 days, present your stamped diary receipt directly to the SDM or CDO.
