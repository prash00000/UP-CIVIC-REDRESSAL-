30-Day Follow-Up Plan

0
Day 0: Submission & Receipt

Send email or hand-deliver printed letter. Ensure you obtain a physical diary receipt number or email delivery acknowledgment.

7
Day 7: First Follow-Up & CUG Inquiry

Call the designated officer's official CUG number during office hours (10:00 AM - 12:00 PM). Quote your diary receipt number and inquire about the inspection schedule.

15
Day 15: Samadhan Diwas Escalation

If no inspection or action has occurred, present your receipt copy at your local Tehsil during Sampoorna Samadhan Diwas (held on designated Saturdays, check your tehsil schedule) to the SDM or CDO.

30
Day 30: Formal Jansunwai Reminder & RTI

If still unresolved past the statutory 30-day timeframe under the UP Janhit Guarantee Act, submit a formal reminder on jansunwai.up.nic.in and file an RTI for the Action Taken Report (ATR).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this an official government website?
No. This is an independent, open citizen tool created to help residents of Uttar Pradesh draft formal complaint letters and identify local administrative contacts. We do not represent any government department.
What happens after I send this complaint letter?
Under the UP Janhit Guarantee Act, 2011, basic civic complaints carry a notified timeframe (typically 7 to 30 days). Always obtain a diary receipt number so you can track progress and escalate during Sampoorna Samadhan Diwas if needed.
Does this tool store my personal information?
No. All text generation and storage happen strictly inside your own browser (using localStorage on your device). Nothing is transmitted to external servers or third-party trackers.
What should I do if an officer's phone number is unreachable or outdated?
Click 'Report a wrong number' on the officer card to notify our maintenance repository, and verify alternative contacts directly on your district's official NIC portal ({district}.nic.in).

This site helps you write letters. It is not run by or connected to the Government of Uttar Pradesh, and it cannot guarantee a response. Officer details can change, so confirm them before you rely on them.
