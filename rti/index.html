Right to Information (RTI) Request Builder

ℹ️ Important: RTI is for public records, not complaints

The Right to Information Act, 2005 is strictly for inspecting and obtaining certified copies of existing government records (such as Measurement Books, muster rolls, technical sanctions, or payment vouchers). If you need an issue fixed, use the complaint tool on the home page.
